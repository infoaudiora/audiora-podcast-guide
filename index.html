How Creators Can Turn Their YouTube Videos Into Podcast Episodes

If you already publish talking-head videos, interviews or tutorials, you are sitting on a podcast you haven't released yet. Plenty of people never watch video at all but happily listen during workouts, chores and long drives. Repurposing your existing uploads as audio lets you reach that crowd without recording anything new.

Pick the Right Videos

Start with content that makes sense without pictures. Conversations, commentary, Q&A sessions and storytelling pieces all work well. Screen-recorded tutorials usually don't, unless you describe each step out loud as you go.

Extract the Audio

You can export audio from your editing software, but if the project files are long gone, the published upload is often the quickest source. A browser tool like Audiora pulls the soundtrack from your own video and saves it as an MP3 in a few seconds.

Tidy It Up for Listeners

Cut any moments that say "as you can see on screen", trim long intros, and add a short spoken opening that explains what the episode covers. Levelling the volume in a free editor such as Audacity makes a noticeable difference on earbuds.

Publish and Promote

Upload the finished file to a podcast host, which will distribute it to Spotify, Apple Podcasts and other apps. Link each episode back to the original video, and mention the podcast in your video descriptions so both audiences can find each other. Releasing on a steady schedule, even once a fortnight, helps new listeners settle into the habit of tuning in.

Only Repurpose What You Own

This approach is meant for your own channel. If guests appear in a video, let them know the conversation will also be released as audio.
